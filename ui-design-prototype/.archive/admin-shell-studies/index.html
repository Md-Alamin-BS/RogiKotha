Three visual systems

Same shell, same markup, same content. Only the stylesheet changes. Each variant answers the four complaints — flat colour, uniform gaps, no focal point, buttons that look like badges — through a different primary mechanism, so you can see which one you actually want.

What was measured wrong, and what every variant fixes

The surface ramp was roughly half the strength of every shipped system.
Light page→card was 1.042:1 (ΔL* 1.68) where Primer ships 1.065, Carbon 1.100, Polaris 1.129. Dark was worse: 1.055:1 where Primer ships 1.093 and Carbon 1.196 — a light-mode-sized step used in dark mode. The hairline sat at 1.14:1 against Primer's 1.43. All three separation mechanisms were too weak at once.
Gaps did not group, because they were all the same size.
Lane boundary vs row gap was 1.58× — on Kubovy & Wagemans' ambiguous floor — and then cancelled outright, because .lane-title and .c-row carried the identical hairline. And .split gap = .detail padding = .lane margin = 26px: three nesting levels on one value. The largest whitespace on the page was 90px of nothing at the bottom edge.
The type ramp was narrower than one type step.
34 of 38 text runs (89%) sat inside a 3.0px band — a 1.29× total ramp. --ink-faint carried 39% of all text at 4.05:1, below AA everywhere it landed. And --accent and --ink-soft were literally the same colour: OKLCH L 0.459 vs 0.458, mutual contrast 1.02.
Nothing said "you can press this".
The queue rendered 36 pill-shaped objects and 29 of them were not interactive. .opt (a button) and .count (a span) shared radius 999px, background and border — and the read-only one rendered heavier. Selection (1.092) was weaker than hover (1.081). There was no commit button anywhere.

A — Chart Tray

surface-led

Structure belongs to the planes, not the lines. Squint until the text blurs and you still see the queue, the selection, the panel and the read-only wells as four distinct materials.

  • Four planes; dark steps 1.74× the light steps
  • Elevation encodes agency — below the page = you cannot act on it
  • Each lane becomes a tray; boundary air 104px vs 16px inside = 6.5:1
  • Hairlines cut from ~19 per screen to 8

Cost: light mode is ~40% weaker than dark (white is a ceiling), the summary clamps to one line, and it dies in forced-colors where backgrounds are stripped.

Open variant A

B — Ink Ledger

type-led

Rank everything by how much ink it costs. One law carries interactivity: a box means you can press it, and nothing that is not a control gets a box.

  • Type ramp 28/19/15/12.5 — a 2.24× span, up from 1.29×
  • Four ink rungs, gaps ΔL* 10 / 20 / 6
  • Badges lose their box entirely — text plus a 6px mark
  • Hover changes the ground; selection changes the ink

Cost: it leans on the font stack, and no webfonts ship. On Nirmala UI the Bengali weight ladder collapses from five steps to two — size and ink have to carry it.

Open variant B

C — Signal Ward

colour-led

Colour is a budget spent only on consequence. Geometry says whether you can press it; colour says what it means.

  • Accent chroma raised 2.7× and moved to a real blue
  • Red and amber collide under deuteranopia at every legible pair — so severity rides on fill inversion + glyph + rail width, never hue
  • Triage rails: 4px red / 2px grey / 2px blue
  • Exactly two saturated objects on screen, never adjacent

Cost: the loudest of the three, and the easiest to break — one more coloured thing and the budget is spent. Needs OKLCH support.
