## Residual risk / decisions for the human

1. **ISS-201 needs a design decision.** Options: (a) a deduplicating queue policy (`exclusive` dedupes key-less sends too, and existing `standard` queues would need migrating); (b) `singletonSeconds`; or (c) accept duplicates and make every handler idempotent as 03 §5 already demands, then correct the comment and test. Alternatively the human may prefer to send T-009 back now rather than carry it as a follow-up.
2. Shutdown worst case: `startPoller.stop()` waits up to 10 s for an in-flight tick, and `queue.stop()` then waits up to 10 s more. That could exceed the 10 s budget if a tick hangs. Observed: 16 ms.
3. `ISS-144`/`ISS-185` (at-most-once claim) remain open. `claimBatch` marks rows processed before enqueue, so a crash between the two loses events.
4. Ctrl-C/SIGTERM delivery was not observed on this Windows host (the IPC path was); worth a manual look at the Phase 0 gate.
5. pg-boss maintenance ran for over 60 s as `rk_worker` with no `queue.error`. Longer runs, such as pg-boss 12.30's vacuum-health monitoring, were not observed.
