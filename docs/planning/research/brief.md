# RogiKotha Gate 1 technical research brief

- Research date: 2026-08-29
- Decision served: establish current, evidence-backed architecture options and identify constraints that require founder confirmation before architecture or backlog approval.
- Research type: technical, with a select decision shape.
- Topology: breadth-first across frontend/UX, trust-and-file security, and operations/policy/model tooling.
- Evidence policy: current primary sources only for technical claims; government, standards-body, official documentation, and original repository sources preferred.
- Validation: spot-check load-bearing claims and record uncertainty where an independent primary source is unavailable.
- Hard gates: open-source-first, commercially usable licensing, self-hostability, accessibility, Bangladesh-first bilingual operation, secure evidence handling, solo-founder maintainability, and a credible migration path.
- Weighted preferences: low operational burden, agent-friendly TypeScript boundaries, reversible choices, predictable cost, mobile performance, and long-term maintainability.
- Exclusions: repository requirements are decision context, not evidence that an external technical or legal claim is true.
