## The alias-isolation choice (CR-012 step 1)

Evidence: in the prototype, r2, r8, r12, r15 and r23 have `identity: 'anon'`, author "Anonymous";
r4 is `pseudo`, author `r_ahmed`. In the corpus each anonymous review has its own persona
`anon:<review>` and account `anon_author_<review>`, which owns nothing else. So no anonymous review's
author also writes public content, and step 1's premise is not met by the data. Making a public
author anonymous would invent a link the prototype does not state (FD-039).

Choice (functional-design Question 1, answer A): **r2, `anon_author_r2`**. r2 is on d1, the doctor
storyboard steps 4 and 6 open; it carries no moderation case; its thread has comments by other
accounts (k11, k12). `seed.int.test.ts` proves that `anon_author_r2` has exactly one persona; that
persona is linked to r2, authors r2 only, and appears on no comment, facility review or reaction;
and the account has no reaction.

`anon_author_r4` exists again, as k16's commenter (the port's thread rule, as `anon_author_r6` for k5).
It is not r4's author, and `cr-012-corpus.test.ts` says so (`ISS-263`).
