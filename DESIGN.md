## Selected direction

**04b — Quiet Clinic (minimal pass).** Paper base, teal-gray accent, hairline
rules, no decorative linework, no atmospheric veil, no panel shadow.
Distributions render as separate outline-only capsules so they cannot be read
as a single quality score. FD-027 supersedes FD-026 (Concept 04 warm variant).

Two artefacts are live, and they no longer share a token set:

- **the patient prototype** at
  `ui-design-prototype/concepts/concept-04b-quiet-clinic-minimal/` — 04b. The

  it.
- **the staff console** at `ui-design-prototype/admin/` — its own surface
  system, *Chart Tray*, under **The staff console** below. FD-030

### Dark mode (04b)

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `hsl(200 14% 8%)` | Deep slate-charcoal, never black |
| `--ink` | `hsl(40 14% 94%)` | Body text — warm cream on slate |
| `--ink-soft` | `hsl(200 6% 72%)` | Secondary text |
| `--ink-faint` | `hsl(200 6% 56%)` | Tertiary text |
| `--accent` | `hsl(190 32% 70%)` | Cooler teal for dark |
| `--accent-2` | `hsl(190 32% 78%)` | Hover |
| `--shadow` | `0 1px 1px hsl(0 0% 0% / .25)` | Same one-pixel shadow, now black |
| `--veil` | `none` | **No atmospheric veil in 04b** |
| `--field-opacity` | `0` | **No living field** |

### Material rules that survive across directions

- No glow shadows. Ever. If depth is needed it is a hairline border or one
  pixel of shadow at low opacity.
- Coral is reserved strictly for emergency and danger so the accent never
  competes with ordinary actions.
- Distribution denominators are always present and never styled to look
  decorative.
- "Not provided" / "No documents" are styled with `--ink-faint`, never with
  red or with strikethrough, never with `0`.

### Font stack

|---|---|---|
| `--serif` | `ui-rounded, 'SF Pro Rounded', 'Segoe UI Variable Text', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` | **Headings in 04b.** Quiet sans, roundness carried by `ui-rounded`. |
| `--sans` | `'Inter', 'Segoe UI Variable Display', 'Segoe UI', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` | UI text, controls |

**No webfonts.** Ship by rule. Bengali must render without a network request.

**Cap drop in Bangla.** The Latin `text-transform: uppercase` is dropped for
Bengali — caps do not exist in the script. Letter-spacing is also dropped.

## Layout

### Radii (04b)

- `--r-sm`: 10px (small chips, small panels)
- `--r-md`: 16px (callouts, controls)
- `--r-lg`: 22px (large panels, hero cards)
- Pills: `999px` for tabs, badges, buttons

Roundness is **carried by shape**, not by a contrasting typeface. The
`ui-rounded` generic face is the progressive enhancement; on Linux/older
Android it falls through to `Segoe UI`/`system-ui` and the radii and pill
shapes still carry the calm.

### Reading measure

`--measure: 72ch` (04b default). Long patient stories cap at 66ch–72ch so the
line length is comfortable in both Latin and Bangla. Provenance lives in the
margin (`margin-note`), not interrupting the text.

### Spacing rhythm

- Inner panel padding: `clamp(18px, 2.8vw, 30px)`
- Outer main padding: `clamp(18px, 3.5vw, 40px)`
- Section margin: `34px` above `.section-head`, `18px` below
- Inter-row gap: `9px–12px`

Varied rhythm — not a single 16/24/32 grid. Tighter groupings inside a card,
generous between cards.

### Distribution capsules (04b)

Each capsule has its own count, label, and outcome tint dot at top-right.
They are rendered as outline-only separate elements so they cannot be read
as a single quality score. `border-radius: var(--r-sm)` or `999px`. The
warm directions (03, 04) used a sentence-of-counts with a spectrum rule
underneath; 04b uses capsules only.

## Components

### Buttons

- Primary: filled with `--accent`, white text, 999px pill, 12px 26px padding,
  one-pixel shadow at low opacity. Press: `--accent-2`, `translateY(-2px)`.
- Ghost: transparent, `--ink-soft` text, `1px solid var(--rule)`. Hover:
  border becomes `--accent`.
- Small: 8px 16px padding, 13px text.

### Badges

- Inline-flex, 11.5px / 600, `1px solid currentColor`, 999px pill.
- Status dot at left (`::before`, 7×7, currentColor).
- `.positive`, `.mixed`, `.negative`, `.plain`, `.warn`, `.danger` variants.
- The dot is hidden for `.plain` / `.warn` / `.danger` — they carry meaning
  in the colour alone.

### Tabs

- `.tabs a`: 13px / 600, 7px 12px padding, 999px pill, transparent border.
- Active: `--accent` text on `--accent-soft` background, accent border at
  40% alpha.
- Staff tab: `.staff` modifier adds a ◆ glyph and `--warn-ink` colour.

### Controls (language, theme, motion, back)

- 36×36 square, `1px solid var(--rule)`, 11px radius, `var(--glass-2)` bg.
- Hover: border becomes `--accent`, text becomes `--accent`,
  `translateY(-2px)`.
- Active (`aria-pressed="true"`): same colours, `--accent-soft` background.

### Panel

- Background: `--glass` (which is `hsl(0 0% 100%)` in 04b — opaque).
- `1px solid var(--rule)` border.
- `--r-lg` (22px) radius.
- `var(--shadow)` — one pixel at low opacity. No glow.
- Padding: `clamp(18px, 2.8vw, 30px)`.

### Field / particle field

- The decorative field was **on in 03**; it is **off in 04b**
  (`.field` / `--field-opacity: 0`).
- 04b deliberately removes it — the calm is carried by surface, not depth.

### Veil

- The atmospheric radial-gradient veil was **on in 03/04**;
  **`--veil: none` in 04b**.
- 04b deliberately removes it — `display: none` on `.veil`.

### Linework

- Decorative SVG linework was carried by the warm variants.
- **Hidden in 04b** (`display: none`) — minimal pass removes it.

## Motion

### Tokens

- `--ease: cubic-bezier(.2, .9, .25, 1)` — used everywhere
- `--dur: 460ms` — default

### Primitives (04b)

- Hero rise — `translateY(14px) → 0` + opacity, on view mount
- First-screen stagger — sequence applied to first viewport items
- Capsule hover — left rule grows
- Tab underline — slides to active
- Button press — `translateY(-2px)`, accent-2 background, deeper shadow

No pulsing dots, no marquees, no bounce/elastic easing, no auto-scroll.
Hover-scale on images is never used.

### Motion off

`body[data-motion="off"]` and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` both kill all
motion with `transition-duration: 1ms !important` and `animation: none`. Every
state is still reachable; only decoration stops.

## The staff console — Chart Tray

The console at `ui-design-prototype/admin/` is a separate artefact with its own
stylesheet and its own tokens. It shares the data layer, the Bengali font stack
and the product rules with 04b, and nothing else. FD-030 (2026-09-08); chosen
from three shell studies built against identical markup — A *Chart Tray*
(surface-led), B *Ink Ledger* (type-led), C *Signal Ward* (colour-led).

### Elevation encodes agency

The one rule a moderator learns:

| Plane | Means |
|---|---|
| Below the page — `--surface-sunken` | You cannot act on it: audit log, evidence viewer |
| The page — `--surface-page` | Ground |
| Raised — `--surface-raised` | Workable: the lane trays, the detail tray |
| Overlay — `--surface-overlay` | Ring plus scrim; in light there is no headroom above white |

One solid accent fill is the primary action, and there is exactly one per view.

### Planes

| Token | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| `--surface-sunken` | `hsl(40 26% 91%)` | `hsl(200 20% 6.5%)` |
| `--surface-page` | `hsl(40 24% 95.5%)` | `hsl(200 15% 11%)` |
| `--surface-raised` | `hsl(0 0% 100%)` | `hsl(200 13% 16%)` |
| `--surface-overlay` | `hsl(0 0% 100%)` | `hsl(200 12% 20%)` |

Light lowers the page rather than lifting the card, because white is a ceiling.
Page-to-raised measures 1.094:1 light and 1.171:1 dark — **the dark steps run
about 1.7× the light steps**, because a tint that works on paper all but
vanishes on near-black, where ambient light floods the dark end and contrast
sensitivity falls away. Shipped systems run the same asymmetry: Primer 1.81×,
Carbon 2.08×, Polaris 2.19×.

A lane is not a heading with an underline — it is a rectangle on a different
plane. Group boundary means plane change, so every hairline a plane can replace
is deleted. State tints (`--surface-hover`, `--surface-selected`) are never a
plane change; selection sits at 1.173 against hover at 1.078, so the selected
row is no longer the weaker of the two.

### Badges are not buttons

The previous stylesheet rendered the read-only count heavier than the
interactive option button. Three channels now separate them, and all three
survive greyscale and Bengali:

| | Badge | Button |
|---|---|---|
| Height | 20px | 34px (`.opt`, 32px) |
| Radius | `--r-badge`, 4px | `--r-control`, 9px |
| Fill | `--fill-badge` — the weakest fill in the system | `--fill-secondary`, or a solid accent for the primary |
| Border | `--rule` | `--border-control` at 4.69:1 |
| Cursor | default, no hover | pointer, hover changes the ground |

`999px` survives only on the count pill, so full-round becomes a unique signal.

### The dock

The console had no commit action at all — option buttons, a reason textarea,
and no submit, on the page that exists to record a moderation decision.
**Record decision** now sits in a `.dock` at the foot of the detail tray:
sticky, separated by `--rule-hard` and an upward-only shadow so it never reads
as a floating card, carrying the one solid accent fill at 8.67:1 against the
tray.

### What the console does not take from 04b

- No serif/sans contrast, no `ui-rounded` — one `--sans` stack throughout.
- No 22px radii. Radius encodes size class, descending: `--r-plane` 14px,
  `--r-well` 10px, `--r-control` 9px, `--r-badge` 4px.
- Four type sizes — 11.5 / 13.5 / 17 / 22px — not a display scale.
- Six spacing steps, every ratio at least 1.5, in place of 30 ad-hoc values.
- Accent is never a text colour. It is spent in three places: the solid primary
  fill, the 3px selection flag, focus rings.
- Bengali scopes only `--row-h` (40px → 56px) and line-height. A plane boundary
  is height-invariant, so the grouping survives a 44% taller line box unchanged.

The cost is on the record: light mode is structurally weaker than dark, and the
direction keeps its structure in backgrounds, so it degrades under
`forced-colors`. The stylesheet re-adds the planes as borders there, which is an
admission that the structure was never in the markup.

## Copy rules

- No buzzwords. *transform, empower, unleash, world-class, supercharge,
  seamless* are out.
- No "Built for X. Designed for Y." cadence.
- Tagline says what the product does, not what it philosophically embraces.
- Em-dashes: kept ≤ 2 per page. Bengali typography uses দাঁড়ি (,) or
  কোলন (:) instead.
- "Not provided" / "No documents" / "Limited data" are the only acceptable
  empty-state copy. Never `0`, never `—`, never "N/A".

## Imagery

- No photography of doctors or patients. Provider marks are deterministic
  geometry derived from a name hash (in `app.js`).
- No emoji as icons — the controls use unicode glyphs (`অ`, `◐`, `◌`, `⌘`,
  `◆`) as a deliberate stylistic choice, not as decoration.
- No AI-generated faces anywhere.

## Accessibility

- Body text clears WCAG AA 4.5:1 in both modes and both languages.
- UI boundaries clear 3:1.
- Skip link: `.skip` jumps from top:-60px to top:0 on focus.
- Focus ring: 2px solid `--accent`, 3px offset, 6px radius.
- Bengali script gets its own type ramp — line-height 1.95–2.0, no caps,
  no negative letter-spacing.
- Reduced motion is fully respected.

## Performance

- No webfonts → no FOIT, no network requests.
- No images → no LCP pressure.
- Scroll-driven progress bar uses `@supports (animation-timeline: scroll())`
  with a static fallback.
- Particle field drops to nothing if `2D canvas` is unavailable.

## What this means for trim work

The detector baseline found 32 anti-patterns across the prototype. Most were
**inherited** from the shared component layer that 03/04 used, not specific
to 04b. The trim work was planned as follows:

1. **04b (selected)** — strip the inherited warm tokens and the shared
   layer's `--veil`, `--shadow`, `--field-opacity`, `.linework` declarations
   so the file is true to its intent: paper, teal-gray, no veil, no
   shadow, no field, no linework.
2. **03 (editorial reference)** — keep warm tokens, terracotta anchor,
   spectrum rule. The radial-gradient veils are *intentional* there
   ("two washes rather than three"). The detector's `radial-halo` finding
   is a false positive for the chosen visual world; mark it suppressed.
3. **04 (warm comparator)** — keep warm tokens; the glow shadows and
   radial veils are intentional. Suppress detector findings as
   intentional.
4. **Gallery `index.html`** — fix the four `low-contrast` issues
   (`#ffffff` on `#70a9ff` fails 4.5:1), bump the four `undersized-ui-text`
   10.5px labels up to 11.5px, add inset padding to the cramped sections.

Items 2 and 3 are closed: 03 and 04 are archived, so nothing is trimmed there
any more and the rules that were suppressed for them stay on the record. Live
trim scope is 04b and the staff console.

See `.impeccable/config.json` for the intentional suppresses,
`docs/status/impeccable-detector-baseline.json` for the full baseline, and
`docs/status/impeccable-detector-final.json` for the run after the trim.
