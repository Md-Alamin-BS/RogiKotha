### Summary

The release-1 cut is consistent: every active contract has one building Unit per operation, no consumer was left without a provider, and the three open questions are settled by level-1 Units (U9, U11) or U48 itself before their consumers' Code Generation, so they do not block parallel building. Two seams still force the parallel agents to guess: which review id each seam takes, and how a new module gets its repositories past the closed `CommandPorts`. Both should be pinned before U11, U46, U48 and U49 start in parallel.
