### Summary

The catalogue's ownership is correct against 02-data-model.md and FD-064/FD-065/FD-066. The port inversions of FD-067 are coherent, and the hard rules on facility feedback, restricted reporters and audited original access are respected. The main concern is that the catalogue, which is the source of truth, was not updated after the owner's last three decisions. FD-069's Directory and Moderation deletion steps are missing from the graph. ADR-011 contradicts the catalogue on where Notification gets its expiry, and its version would create a cycle. FD-070 is only partly applied. In addition, ADR-005 claims an anonymity boundary that the catalogue's own entities do not keep. All of these are small edits, but they should land before Units Generation reads this graph.
