### Summary

This is a buildable and careful design. It fits the T-010 boundary matrix and the binding FD rows, and its enumeration, session and key-scope defences are concrete and testable. The one Major gap is that the reset flow cannot enforce "password must not equal email" before the library stores the new password. The Minor findings are a sign-in window range that contradicts its own "every value takes effect" claim, stale decision-log append instructions, a missing network cap on outgoing auth mail, two unspecified registration races, and the worker `Crypto` instance's undefined `hmac`.
