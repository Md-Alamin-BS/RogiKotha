### Summary

The design is sound. Only the author can correct, through `isReviewAuthor`, and every other case gets the same `not_found`. The version actor is the persona, and `actor_id` is never returned. Quick and detailed reviews are both editable. A stale `expectedVersion` is caught under `FOR UPDATE`. The C9 refinements only add to the contract, and every rule has a test. Two points need the owner's attention: whether "the original account stays readable" in AC6.3.2 is met, and the claim of no collision with U48 and U49, which is wrong because the demo files are shared and the "merges last" deletion step has no defined mechanism.
