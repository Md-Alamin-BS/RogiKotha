### Summary

The plan fits release 1 with no CI and no scope creep, and the traceability is complete. But the end-to-end suite as written will not give two clean runs. Tests share data under `fullyParallel` (P2.8 to P2.10, P3.11 to P3.14). The publishing tests change Dr. Farhana Rahman's counts while P1.4 reads them. The hard-rule word pattern matches correct copy. The suite's own config test never runs, and Playwright would load it as a test. All four can be fixed in the design in under an hour. Cutting the unrequired leftover removal, screenshots and tools work frees a large part of the day.
