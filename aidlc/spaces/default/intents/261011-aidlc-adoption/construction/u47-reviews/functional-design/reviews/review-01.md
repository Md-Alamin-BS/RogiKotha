### Summary

The design is thorough and close to buildable. The demo-retirement table matches `main`, the hard rules are covered, and the U46 and U11 assumptions match their designs (`listExperienceCards` params, `addMemberDoctor`, `personaFor` with its deferred key, `publicPersonaIdsByUsername`). Two points should be settled before Code Generation. First, the seams' calling convention (`ctx`, the factory's return type, the web-tier accessor) must be pinned, or U48 and U49 cannot build without touching U47's files. Second, the anonymous publish with a new doctor leaves an account link in `profile_submissions`, and the owner should decide how to handle it.
