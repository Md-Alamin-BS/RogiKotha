Checked and found sound: every command and query declares `requires` as BR1.1, BR1.2 and BR1.5 state. Publish adds `notRestricted`, and `hasOwnReviewOfDoctor` is stricter than the spec. Draft reads and writes filter on `account_id`, and another account's draft or an unknown id gets the same `not_found`. Audit rows use the persona as the actor and only allowlisted detail keys. The outbox payload carries no persona or account. The public projection and `PageBylineVM` carry no internal, account or persona id, no exact date, and no number standing for quality. An anonymous review gets a fresh thread persona, no `otherByAuthor` list, no author link, and its alias shows on the page byline only. Missing sentiment, outcome and period render as "Not provided", and no evidence renders as "No documents", never `0`. Claims render under "Reviewer's label". The index and author page sort newest first. The slots take only `reviewPublicId` and keep a failure inside the slot. Files changed outside the owned paths are additive and listed in `code-summary.md`. The author page shows a named display name beside `@username`, which follows the approved BR7.2 (Question 14), so it is not raised here.

### Summary

The module's authorization, anonymity, audit and public projections hold up against the design, and typecheck, lint, i18n parity and the unit tests all pass. Before approving, the owner should weigh two linked composer problems: Publish can silently publish an older saved version when the last save failed, and drafts containing ordinary Bengali joiner characters (ZWNJ, ZWJ; ISS-530) cannot be saved at all.
