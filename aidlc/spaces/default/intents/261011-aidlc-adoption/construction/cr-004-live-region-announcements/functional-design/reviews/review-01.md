### Summary

The design is implementable: its rules, workflows, message keys and traceability resolve against the code and the shared contracts. The one significant concern is that option A depends on the freshly mounted live region being spoken after a locale switch. The spec leaves the remedy to a file outside the Unit's owned paths, and the owner should settle it at plan approval. The other four findings are small corrections to the spec, tests and traceability.
