### Summary

The design is buildable, stays inside U9's owned paths, keeps FD-103 (register never signs in; the same cookie and redirect for every address) and FD-104 (no `/api/auth/*` calls), and its enumeration and open-redirect handling is sound in principle. The two Major findings are both about the `AuthFormState` contract: rate-limit interpolation, notices and the expired flag are unspecified, and the refill rule contradicts the rule that no address is returned. Settle both before Code Generation.
