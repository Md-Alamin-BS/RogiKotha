### Summary

The design is sound and can be built. Anonymity holds: the author of an anonymous review comments under that thread's alias, and everyone else comments under their pseudonym. Ownership on delete goes through `ownsPersona`, with the same `not_found` for every refusal. The slot keeps U47's frozen props. The rate limit and the bilingual requirements are clear. The two Major points are about U11's approved limits. The author lookup must stay within the 200-id batch limit, and the public `mine` flag needs either an amendment to U11 BR3.4 or a switch to `ownsPersona`. The demo retirement also touches shared files that U48 edits in parallel.
