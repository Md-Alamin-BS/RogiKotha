| R-01 | Major | packages/contracts/src/schemas/community.ts > `commentBody` (used by `PostCommentInput.body`) | The comment body is only trimmed, NFC-normalised and length-checked. It accepts every control and format character, which has three effects you can reach today. (1) A body made only of a zero-width space (U+200B) survives `trim()` (JavaScript does not treat U+200B as whitespace), counts as 1 code point, and is published as an empty-looking comment. (2) A body with NUL (U+0000) passes the schema, but PostgreSQL refuses it on insert. `PgCommunityRepository.insert` rethrows anything that is not the reply-depth raise, so the poster gets `errors.common.internal` instead of a validation message, and the post still counts against the 30-per-hour limit, because `rateLimiter.hit` runs before the insert. Review text refuses all of these through `hasControlOrFormatExceptJoiners` (FD-114: the author's own words may contain ZWNJ and ZWJ, and every other Cc or Cf character is refused). Comments, which are also the author's own words, now follow a looser rule that no decision records. code-summary.md says the gap was "reported to the owner", but none of the ISS-660 to ISS-678 rows in docs/implementation/ISSUES.md records it. | Ask the owner whether comment text follows FD-114. ### Summary

The release-1 community module holds up on the points the gate cares about. Post requires a confirmed, active account. Delete is author-only and gives one `not_found` for not-own, unknown and already-deleted comments. Audit rows name the persona only. The view model carries public ids and persona projections only. A review author's comment on an anonymous review is written under that thread's alias. Moderation-hidden rows and their replies are never listed. Placeholders show only while live replies exist. The one point the owner should decide before approving is the comment body's missing control- and format-character check: it allows invisible comments, turns NUL into an internal error that still counts against the limit, and departs from FD-114 without any recorded decision or ISS row.
