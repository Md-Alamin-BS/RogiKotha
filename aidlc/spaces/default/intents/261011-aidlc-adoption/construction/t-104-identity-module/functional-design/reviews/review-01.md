### Summary

The design holds up on the points that matter for a high-risk identity Unit. Projections carry no ids. Each anonymous alias is random and scoped to one thread, with hue 0, so threads cannot be linked. `ownsPersona` gives one boolean, takes the account id from the actor, and only the review and community modules receive it. The migration applies cleanly to the seeded data, and routing register through the account repository avoids U9's files. The two Major findings, both easy to fix, are:
- the partial unique indexes must be declared in the Drizzle schema, or a later generated migration drops them;
- the `persona_links` allowlist misses `demo/comments.ts`.
