### Summary

The design is thorough, and its library claims hold against the installed Next 16.3.4, next-intl and Better Auth sources. But it keeps the existing page matcher, which skips any path containing a dot, and slugged experience URLs accept dots. A crafted link therefore serves a user-content page with no CSP, no Host check and no proxy Origin rule. Fix the matcher (Critical) and widen the log-scrub inventory to direct console output (Major). The remaining findings are alignment fixes.
