### Summary

Both Majors from the T-201 review are resolved. § 3 and BR7.1 to BR7.3 now say, function by function, what U46 replaces, absorbs or keeps from the demo slice. FD-101's free-text match becomes the one condition rule (BR1.7), and `name_*` keeps its meaning. FD-100's mark is carried by a SQL-derived `memberAddedNotConfirmed` on `DoctorCardVM` and `DoctorProfileVM` (BR2.13), with a database test. The hard rules hold in the data layer and are tested at query level: facility isolation (BR2.6, BR2.7), no percentages or quality numbers (BR2.1), neutral ordering (BR2.8), `registrationNotVerified: true` (BR2.5) and "Not provided" for missing values (BR2.2). The migration is safe on seeded data: every new column is nullable or has a default, and no provisional terms exist yet, so the new partial unique index cannot conflict with existing rows. The kept functions do cover the U20, U22 and U47 pages, and FD rows (a), (b) and (c) are sound, with FD-106 onward free. The one gap to close before Code Generation is the deletion plan: it breaks two test files outside U46's list, one of them the evidence adversarial test.
